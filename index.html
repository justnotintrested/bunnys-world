A letter, by candlelight

Hey Bunny.

If you're reading this, then it's almost time — or it already is. Either way, hello. I've been meaning to say all of this for a while, but somehow words always end up smaller than feelings. So I built you a room instead.

You are one of those people who doesn't quite know how uncommon you are. You feel things in full — not in half-measures, not with disclaimers. The world handed you hard months and you came out of them with your chin up and your nails done. Very Cancer of you. Very you.

NIFT wasn't just a degree. It was proof — that the girl who dreamed in fabric and colour had the discipline to match the vision. You made something of it. You are still making something of it.

Ginju picked the right person. Animals know.

So here is a small dark room of a website, lit just for you. Crimson and gold, because that's how your world has always been coloured. Stay exactly as soft and as fierce as you are.
